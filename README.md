# Community Drug Abuse Awareness Project

This project focuses on raising awareness about drug abuse among youths in our community.

Problem:
Drug abuse is increasing among young people and affecting their health, education, and future.

Solution:
We use community programs, digital awareness campaigns, and educational outreach to educate young people about the dangers of drug misuse.

Impact:
Helping youths make better life choices and creating a healthier community.
